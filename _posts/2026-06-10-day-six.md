##GAIA TAP SERVER WHY

Today ended up being much less productive than I was hoping for. I was working on the TGLC and got two light curves of stars from our catalog that I had already found myself. I finally got a breakthrough and realize the reason the graphs looked so weird was the error bars! I did see one normal light curve and forgot to save it :(. And then... I was unable to make another graph the rest of the day. The best I can figure out is that the GAIA TAPS server is down, and that is why nothing is working. So I do not have a definitive result on whether or not we should use TGLC. I will note that even before it stopped working, the code took a very long time to run, something to be aware of with workflow. So, I switched gears and did some looking into ways to identify flares. There were many options, FBEYE, AltaiPony, etc. However, I think the one we should look into is "stella" from Feinstein 2020. It is a convolutional neural network (CNN). There is some work that shows ML have better outcomes, however at the cost of relative runtime. If not I think AltaiPony would be a good backup option.
