One thing I noticed with my hand-picked sample pool was that large flares were getting removed, which I really want to avoid because I think those are the coolest! So the way I solved that was that random noise spikes are I believe almost all one point wayy outside the MAD, but a large flare like that is going to have two points that are way outside that so it has the flare shape. So I altered the code I wrote that just said if anything is above 7 sigma get rid of it to if it is one point get rid of it, if it is two keep it. And that successfully salvaged the large flares.
